# Melee Combat Action Flow

At the moment the attack roll under the combat tab goes straight into the damage resolution. But the person being attacked need to be allowed to perform a defensive roll before the damage gets resolved.

In a combat round an attack would be towards the character that is targeted in the system. In the situation where an attack succeeds, instead of going straight to the damage resolution, a message should be sent prompting the target to perform a defense roll.

When this roll is performed, the successes from this roll should be subtracted from the attack. If successes falls below 1, the attack has successfully been defended against.

Regardless if the defense roll is performed or not (since the defend roll is optional), there should be an option to resolve the attack by clicking a button. When the button is clicked, the attack moves on to the damage resolution stage.
